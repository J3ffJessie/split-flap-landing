Privacy Policy

Effective: September 18, 2026

Split-Flap Display ("the app") is a desktop application. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what happens to it. There is no backend server operated by the developer that receives or stores your personal data — everything below happens either on your own device or directly between your device and the third-party service named.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar in Settings, the app requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly access — read-only, so the app cannot create, edit, or delete anything in your calendar. This is used only to show your upcoming events on the split-flap board and to preempt the board for urgent events.

  • Sign-in uses Google's standard OAuth flow with PKCE, opened in your system browser.
  • Access and refresh tokens are stored in your operating system's own credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or Linux Secret Service) — never uploaded to a server the developer controls.
  • Calendar event data fetched from the API is used locally to render the board and is not transmitted anywhere else.
  • You can disconnect at any time from the app's Settings, which deletes the stored tokens from your device, or revoke access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Quotes

By default the app fetches quotes from the public ZenQuotes API to display on the board. These requests don't include any personal or account information — they're anonymous requests for quote text. Quotes you add yourself (individually or via bulk import) are stored only in the app's local data folder on your device.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-up with the developer
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No sale or sharing of your data with third parties beyond the Google and ZenQuotes API calls described above, which are required for the features you opt into

Data retention and deletion

Disconnecting Google Calendar in Settings removes the stored tokens immediately. Uninstalling the app removes its local application data, including the quote cache and any locally-added quotes, from your device.

Children's privacy

Split-Flap Display is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
