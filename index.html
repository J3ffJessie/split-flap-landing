Split-Flap Display

A desktop split-flap departure board for your calendar and your favorite quotes.

Split-Flap Display is a free desktop app (Windows, macOS, Linux) that turns your screen into a classic airport-style split-flap board. It rotates through quotes — pulled from a public quotes API or ones you add yourself — and can optionally connect to your Google Calendar so upcoming events preempt the board when they're urgent.

Features

  • Animated split-flap rendering with theming
  • Quote rotation from a public quotes API, plus your own local quotes (single or bulk import)
  • Windowed, widget, and fullscreen display modes
  • Optional Google Calendar integration — urgent events preempt the board

Your data stays on your device

Split-Flap Display doesn't run its own backend and doesn't have user accounts. If you connect Google Calendar, your sign-in tokens are stored in your operating system's own credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or Linux Secret Service) — never on a server we run. See the Privacy Policy for details.
