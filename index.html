Split-flap-desktop

What it is: a free desktop app that turns your screen into a classic airport-style split-flap board, showing your calendar and favorite quotes.

Split-flap-desktop is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux. It renders an animated split-flap (airport departure board style) display on your screen. By default it rotates through quotes — pulled from a public quotes API or ones you add yourself. You can optionally connect your Google Calendar so upcoming events are shown on the board and urgent events preempt the current quote.

Features

  • Animated split-flap rendering with theming
  • Quote rotation from a public quotes API, plus your own local quotes (single or bulk import)
  • Windowed, widget, and fullscreen display modes
  • Optional Google Calendar integration — urgent events preempt the board

Your data stays on your device

Split-flap-desktop doesn't run its own backend and doesn't have user accounts. If you connect Google Calendar, your sign-in tokens are stored in your operating system's own credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or Linux Secret Service) — never on a server we run. See the Privacy Policy for details.
